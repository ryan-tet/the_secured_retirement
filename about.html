Choosing a life insurance agent: common questions

How do I choose a life insurance agent in Florida?

Start with three checks. First, confirm the agent’s license with the Florida Department of Financial Services. Second, ask if they are independent, so they can compare several insurance companies instead of selling just one. Third, ask for side-by-side illustrations that show guaranteed and non-guaranteed values, total premiums, surrender charges and how policy loans work. A good agent explains all of it in plain English and never rushes you.
