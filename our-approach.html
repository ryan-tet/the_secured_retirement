A THOUGHTFUL FRAMEWORK

Five Pillars of a Resilient Retirement

A retirement plan should balance protection, income, taxes, and growth around what matters to you. Explore the principles behind our approach.

Principal Protection

Help safeguard your retirement savings from major market losses.

Learn how it works

Downside floor strategies and fixed indexed buffers can limit exposure to specified market losses, subject to their terms and limits. Capital preservation approaches aim to help keep a portion of savings available for future needs.

Diversification Strategies

Reduce risk with a balanced approach beyond traditional stocks and bonds.

Learn how it works

Multi-asset correlation management considers how holdings may respond differently to changing conditions. Alternatives and tactical allocation can complement—not replace—a suitable core plan beyond a traditional 60/40 split.

Guaranteed Income Options

Create a steady income stream for retirement you’ve worked so hard for.

Learn how it works

Lifetime income options may create an income floor to help cover essential living expenses regardless of market volatility. Guarantees depend on the issuing institution’s claims-paying ability and the contract terms.

Tax-Efficient Solutions

Keep more of what you earn with smart, tax-advantaged strategies.

Learn how it works

Roth conversion ladders, tax-bracket management, and required minimum distribution (RMD) optimization can help coordinate taxable income over time. Tax consequences are individual; consult a qualified tax professional.

Long-Term Growth

Build and protect your wealth for the next chapter of your life.

Learn how it works

Once principal and essential income needs are addressed, a plan can preserve appropriate upside participation for long-term goals. The allocation and potential tradeoffs depend on your time horizon and risk tolerance.

PREPARE WITH PURPOSE

The Next Bear Market Could Be Closer Than You Think

History shows that bear markets are a normal part of the investment cycle. The real question isn’t if it will happen — it’s how prepared you’ll be when it does.

Market downturns can reduce your account value.

A 40% portfolio loss requires a 67% gain just to break even. When you are actively withdrawing retirement income, sequence-of-returns risk can permanently deplete your nest egg.

Recovery Asymmetry -40% Drop = +67% Needed

Inflation can erode your purchasing power.

Even moderate 3.5% annual inflation cuts the real value of fixed cash savings in half over 20 years—quietly straining healthcare, housing, and lifestyle budgets.

20-Year Real Value Impact -50% Purchasing Power

Traditional portfolios may not be enough.

Conventional stock-and-bond mixes often suffer when interest rate shifts cause equities and fixed income to decline simultaneously—leaving retirees without a true shock absorber.
